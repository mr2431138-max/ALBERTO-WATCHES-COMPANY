Watch And Clock Technology

Learn about the mechanisms and features that keep time moving.

Mechanical Movement

Mechanical watches use a mainspring and gears to measure time. Many enthusiasts value their intricate engineering and traditional craftsmanship.

Quartz Movement

Quartz watches use a battery and a vibrating quartz crystal to provide reliable and accurate everyday timekeeping.

Solar Power

Solar-powered watches convert light into energy and can reduce the need for routine battery replacement.

Clock Mechanisms

Clocks may use quartz movements powered by batteries or mechanical mechanisms powered by springs and gears. These mechanisms help clocks display accurate time.

Smart Watch Features

Depending on the model, smart watches may provide notifications, activity tracking, health-related features and smartphone connectivity.

Wall Clock Technology

Wall clocks are available in modern quartz and traditional mechanical designs. They combine timekeeping with decorative styles suitable for homes, offices and other spaces.

Customer Support

Helpful information for taking care of your watch and clock.

How should I care for my watch?

Keep your watch clean, avoid impacts and follow the manufacturer's instructions for cleaning, water resistance and servicing.

Can every watch be used in water?

No. Water resistance differs by model. Always check the manufacturer's specifications before exposing a watch to water.

When should a watch be serviced?

Service intervals depend on the movement and manufacturer. Consult the official manual or a qualified watch technician.

How should I care for my clock?

Keep your clock in a clean, dry place away from direct sunlight, dust and extreme temperatures. Replace batteries when needed, handle delicate parts carefully, and arrange professional servicing if the clock stops or keeps incorrect time.
